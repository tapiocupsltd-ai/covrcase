Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 2026

The short version: we only collect your name and email when you join our waitlist. We use it to notify you about our launch. We never sell your data. You can unsubscribe any time.

1. Who We Are

COVR Ireland is an Irish company operating automated phone case printing kiosks in retail locations across Ireland. Our website is covrcases.com and you can contact us at hello@covrcases.com.

For the purposes of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Irish data protection law, COVR Ireland is the data controller.

2. What Data We Collect

We collect the following personal data when you sign up to our waitlist:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • The date and time of your submission

We also collect standard website analytics data (such as pages visited, time on site, and approximate location by country) through our analytics provider. This data is aggregated and does not identify you personally.

We do not collect any sensitive personal data, payment information, or data from minors under 16.

3. How We Use Your Data

We use your name and email address for the following purposes:

  • To notify you when COVR launches at its first location
  • To send you exclusive launch-week offers if you have opted in
  • To send occasional updates about COVR (no more than once per month pre-launch)

We will never use your data for any purpose other than those listed above without asking for your explicit consent first.

4. Legal Basis for Processing

Under GDPR, we process your personal data on the basis of consent — you voluntarily provided your details by signing up to our waitlist. You have the right to withdraw this consent at any time by unsubscribing.

5. Who We Share Your Data With

We use the following third-party services to process your data:

  • Formspree — processes waitlist form submissions. Formspree is GDPR-compliant. View their privacy policy at formspree.io.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data with any third party for marketing purposes. Ever.

6. How Long We Keep Your Data

We will retain your name and email address until you unsubscribe or request deletion, or for a maximum of 24 months from the date you signed up — whichever comes first. After this period, your data will be permanently deleted.

7. Your Rights Under GDPR

As an individual based in the EU/EEA or Ireland, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right of access — you can request a copy of the data we hold about you
  • Right to rectification — you can ask us to correct inaccurate data
  • Right to erasure — you can ask us to delete your data at any time
  • Right to restrict processing — you can ask us to limit how we use your data
  • Right to data portability — you can request your data in a machine-readable format
  • Right to object — you can object to us processing your data
  • Right to withdraw consent — you can unsubscribe at any time using the link in any email we send you

To exercise any of these rights, email us at hello@covrcases.com and we will respond within 30 days.

8. Cookies

Our website uses only essential cookies necessary for the site to function. We do not use advertising cookies or tracking cookies that follow you across other websites. If we add analytics in the future, we will update this policy and, where required, ask for your consent.

9. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data from unauthorised access, loss, or disclosure. Our website is served over HTTPS at all times.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Continued use of our website after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

11. Contact & Complaints

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your data, please contact us:

  • Email: hello@covrcases.com
  • Website: covrcases.com

If you are not satisfied with our response, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the Irish Data Protection Commission (DPC) at dataprotection.ie or by calling +353 57 868 4800.
