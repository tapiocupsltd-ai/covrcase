How It Works

From photo to case
in three simple steps

No app download. No waiting days for delivery. No complicated ordering process. Just you, your phone, and 3 minutes.

01
📱

Scan the QR Code

Open your phone camera and scan the QR code on the kiosk. Upload your favourite photo — from camera roll, Instagram, or anywhere.

02
💳

Choose & Pay

Select your phone model, adjust the design, and tap to pay with card or contactless. €25 for a standard case, €30 for a magnetic case.

03
🎨

Collect Your Case

Watch the machine print your case in real time. In 3 minutes flat, collect your premium custom case from the dispenser.

The Product

Premium UV print.
Every time.

Not a sticker. Not a paper sleeve. UV-cured ink bonded directly to the case — vivid, scratch-resistant, and built to last. Custom cases online cost €40+ and take weeks. Ours print in 3 minutes for €25.
