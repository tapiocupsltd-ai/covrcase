Before you visit

Your questions, answered

How much does a custom phone case cost?

A standard custom phone case costs €25 and a magnetic case costs €30. Pay by card or contactless at the kiosk.

How long does printing take?

Printing takes around 3 minutes. Allow extra time to choose your phone model, upload your photo, adjust the design and pay.

Which phones are supported?

COVR supports models in the iPhone 14–17, Samsung Galaxy S23–S25 and Google Pixel 7–9 ranges. Exact variants and stock can differ; check your model and case type at the kiosk before paying, or email hello@covrcases.com before travelling.

What is the difference between standard and magnetic cases?

The standard case costs €25; the magnetic option costs €30. Check that the magnetic case offered for your exact phone suits your accessories before paying. Contact hello@covrcases.com if you need help confirming compatibility.

What photo should I use?

Choose a clear, well-lit original photo. Avoid blurry images or small screenshots. Check the crop and keep important faces or details away from the camera cut-out when adjusting your design.

Do I need an app or an online order?

No app download or online order is needed. Visit the kiosk at Marina Market, scan its QR code with your phone camera and follow the steps on screen.

Where can I find COVR in Cork?

Visit Marina Market, Centre Park Road, Cork, T12 YX76. The listed opening hours are Monday to Sunday, 8am–8pm. The directions link takes you to Marina Market.

What if I need help with my case or printing?

Email hello@covrcases.com with your phone model, the time of your visit and a description of the problem so the COVR team can help.
