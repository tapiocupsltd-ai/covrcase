Finding the kiosk

By the Graffiti Room

Once you're inside Marina Market, head for the Graffiti Room. The COVR kiosk is right beside it — look for the blue COVR branding and sample cases on display. Scan the QR code on the front to start.

Getting here

Parking, access & dogs

Parking on-site

Marina Market has its own car park on Centre Park Road in Cork's docklands, so you can drive in, print your case and go.

Accessible

The market has toilet facilities with disabled access.

Dog-friendly

Bring the dog. There are water stations around the market, and a photo of your dog makes a great case.

Check Marina Market's visitor information for the latest on parking and facilities.

Why print it here

Your case prints while you eat

Marina Market has over 35 food and drink vendors under one roof. Start your design, then grab something from Oakfire Pizza, Hana Korean BBQ or Panda's Matcha, or browse Jackie Brown Vintage and Kawaii Store. Your case is ready in minutes, not days, and there's no delivery to wait in for.

Coming for an event? Marina Market runs weekly events, plus DogFest in the summer, Christmas Markets and Cork on Ice. A custom case with a photo from the day makes a good souvenir or last-minute gift. See the events calendar.

Made while you visit

From your photo to your case

1. Scan and upload

Scan the QR code on the kiosk and upload your photo. No app download is needed.

2. Choose and pay

Check your exact phone model, choose your case, adjust the design and pay by card or contactless.

3. Collect your case

The kiosk prints directly onto the case using UV-cured ink. Printing takes around 3 minutes; allow extra time for designing and payment.

Supported ranges: iPhone 14–17, Samsung Galaxy S23–S25 and Google Pixel 7–9. Check your phone model.

Visiting Marina Market

Questions about this location

Where is the phone case kiosk in Marina Market?

The COVR kiosk is inside Marina Market, by the Graffiti Room. Look for the blue COVR-branded printing machine.

Can I get a custom phone case the same day in Cork?

Yes. The kiosk prints your case while you wait. Printing takes around 3 minutes; allow a few extra minutes to pick your model, upload your photo, adjust the design and pay.

Is there parking at Marina Market?

Yes, Marina Market has parking on-site on Centre Park Road, so you can drive in, print your case and head off.

Can I bring my dog?

Yes. Marina Market is dog-friendly and has water stations for dogs around the market.

What does it cost and how do I pay?

A standard case costs €25 and a magnetic case costs €30. Pay by card or contactless at the kiosk; no app or online order is needed.

When can I print a case?

Marina Market lists its opening hours as Monday to Sunday, 8am–8pm. Email hello@covrcases.com if you're travelling a long way and want to confirm your phone model first.
